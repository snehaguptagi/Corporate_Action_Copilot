═══ A. GIVE THE EVENT REAL OPTIONS ═══
Section 03 currently has ONE row: "Exercise Default / Subscribe. / Rights × ₹85".
A rights issue has three choices and the product must model all three, because
the comparison between them IS the decision:

  Exercise           subscribe at ₹85. Costs cash, keeps your holding whole.
  Sell entitlement   sell the Rights Entitlement on NSE/BSE during the RE
                     window. Recovers value without funding. RE trades under
                     its own ISIN and the window closes BEFORE the issue does.
  Let lapse          do nothing. Costs you the full value of the entitlement.

For each option show the CONSEQUENCE in rupees, not a formula:
  Exercise         "Pay ₹22.44 cr, receive 26,40,000 shares"
  Sell             "Recover about ₹7.70 cr, no funding needed"
  Lapse            "Forfeit ₹7.70 cr"

The economics, once, so the numbers are checkable:
  TERP = (5 × ₹120 + ₹85) / 6 = ₹114.1667
  value of one right = ₹114.1667 − ₹85 = ₹29.1667
  26,40,000 rights × ₹29.1667 = ₹7.70 cr
That ₹7.70 cr forfeit is the most important number in this event and currently
cannot be displayed anywhere. Fix that.

Also fix the rendering: "Exercise Default" is a label and a default-badge
colliding into one string. And "Subscribe." is not a description.
