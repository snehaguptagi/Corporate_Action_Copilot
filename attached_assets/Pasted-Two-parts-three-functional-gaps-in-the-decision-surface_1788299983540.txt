Two parts: three functional gaps in the decision surface, then a design pass.
The design pass is mostly REMOVING inconsistency, not adding decoration.

═══ PART 1 — FINISH THE DECISION ═══

1. THE PAGE TELLS YOU TO SELL, THEN DEFAULTS TO FORFEITING.
   Both constraint messages say "sell the remaining 7,72,993 on exchange before
   the RE window closes". But the live totals read:
       Sell 0 rights
       Value forfeited ₹2.51 cr
   So the default decision throws away ₹2.51 cr that the page's own guidance
   says to recover. For a constrained scheme, the remainder above the permitted
   maximum must DEFAULT to "sell entitlement", not lapse. Forfeiting should
   never be a default — it is the one choice that destroys value.

2. PER-SCHEME CHOICE IS MISSING.
   Section 03 describes three options at event level (exercise / sell
   entitlement / let lapse), but each scheme row in section 05 only offers
   "Exercise" plus a quantity. There are no select controls in the DOM. A fund
   manager must be able to choose per scheme — exercise some, sell the rest,
   lapse a small odd lot — because that is exactly what the constraints force.
   Add the choice per scheme row, and let a single scheme split across exercise
   and sell.

3. THE INPUT max IS THE WRONG NUMBER.
   Small Cap renders value="211764" max="300000".
   Focused 25 renders value="1027007" max="1800000".
   The server correctly refuses anything above the permitted maximum, so
   nothing breaks — but the browser lets you type past the cap and only tells
   you after submitting. Set max to the permitted maximum, and keep the server
   check. Belt and braces, not braces only.

═══ PART 2 — MAKE IT CONSISTENT ═══
These are measured from the running app, not opinions.

4. ONE COLOUR SPACE, ONE PALETTE. Right now raw rgb() and raw oklch()/oklab()
   literals are both used in components, so two colour systems coexist:
     rgb(42, 34, 29)                    warm near-black ink
     oklch(0.985 0.001 106.423)         warm off-white ground
     oklch(0.554 0.046 257.417)         COOL blue-grey  <- clashes
     rgb(75, 27, 47)                    maroon (old sidebar)
     rgb(209, 73, 0)                    orange (primary)
   Warm and cool neutrals sitting next to each other is most of why it reads
   inconsistent, and three accent directions is the rest.

   Do this:
   - Commit to the WARM neutral family already dominant: the warm ink on the
     warm off-white ground. Remove every cool blue-grey.
   - Keep ONE accent. The orange is used 41 times, the maroon twice — keep the
     orange, and retire the maroon or restate it as the darkest warm neutral.
   - Reserve green / amber / red for STATUS ONLY (comfortable, attention,
     breach). Semantic colour is not the accent and must never be used for
     decoration.
   - Every colour comes from a token. No raw rgb() or oklch() literal anywhere
     in a component. 36 tokens are already defined and being bypassed.

5. FIGURES NEED A MONOSPACE AND TABULAR NUMERALS. Measured: no monospace face
   is used anywhere and font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums is not set on a
   single table cell. This product is columns of money and share quantities —
   ₹15.30 cr, 22.00 paise, 26,40,000, 10,27,007 — and they currently do not
   align vertically.
   - Load one monospace face (IBM Plex Mono or JetBrains Mono) and apply it to
     EVERY figure: money, quantities, paise, percentages, dates in tables.
   - Set font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on all of them.
   - Right-align every numeric column. Left-align text columns.
   This one change will do more for legibility than anything else on the list.

6. GIVE IT A REAL TYPE SCALE. Measured distribution: 14px × 96, 12px × 29,
   16px × 22, 24px × 1. One 24px element on the entire page, so there is no
   hierarchy and nothing directs the eye.
   Define five steps and use them consistently, letting weight do work too:
     28  page title
     20  section heading
     16  the sentence statements in "What it is", and key figures
     14  table body and prose
     12  labels, units, secondary metadata (uppercase, letter-spaced)
   The three sentences in section 01 are the most important text in the product
   and are currently the same size as a table cell. Raise them.

7. ONE BORDER RADIUS. Measured: 3px and 4px both in use, plus pills. Pick 4px
   for cards, inputs and badges; keep full-round only for genuine pills.

8. CONSISTENCY SWEEP across every page — Dashboard, Portfolio, scheme detail,
   corporate action, archive, audit:
   - one money format (the shared format.ts exists; make sure nothing bypasses
     it — the archive was showing "₹55,27,125" raw while other pages showed
     "₹15.30 cr");
   - one date format everywhere, "15 Sep 2026 · 15:00 IST";
   - one table style: same header treatment, row height, border colour,
     alignment rules;
   - one card style;
   - section headings: ONE heading per section, not an eyebrow plus a title
     plus a subtitle;
   - "Internal deadline" as a column header is internal phrasing — it is the
     fund manager's own deadline. Rename it.

9. STATE AND FOCUS. Every interactive element needs a visible focus ring that
   meets 3:1 contrast, a hover state, and a disabled state that reads as
   disabled rather than just pale. "Submit to Compliance" must look clearly
   unavailable when a scheme is blocked.

═══ DO NOT ═══
- Do not add charts, sparklines, gradients, or icons-as-decoration.
- Do not restructure any page or rename any route.
- Do not add a dark mode in this pass; make the light theme correct first.
- Do not introduce a second font for headings. One sans plus one mono.

═══ VERIFY ═══
typecheck and tests pass, plus:
- selecting "sell entitlement" on a constrained scheme moves its remainder out
  of "forfeited" and into "sell", and the totals update;
- forfeited value is 0 by default on every scheme;
- input max equals the permitted maximum, and the server still refuses an
  over-limit submission;
- a DOM scan finds no raw rgb()/oklch() colour literal in any component;
- every numeric cell uses the mono face with tabular-nums and is right-aligned;
- at least four distinct font sizes are in use with the page title largest;
- one money format and one date format across all six pages;
- every control has a visible focus state.
