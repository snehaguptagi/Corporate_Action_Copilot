The dashboard content is right. The ORGANISATION is wrong: it is a flat list of
seven equal-weight sections, each with its own heading and subtitle, so nothing
tells the reader what to read first. This step reorganises it and fixes four
bugs found in the current build. No new data, no new charts.

═══ PART 1 — MERGE THE TWO TIME CHARTS INTO ONE ═══
This is the most important change here.

"Decision deadlines, next 30 days" and "Funding due by week" share the SAME
x-axis — time. They are currently two separate full-width sections, and worse,
funding appears BEFORE the deadlines it is derived from.

Combine them into ONE chart with one shared time axis and two stacked rows:
    top row     one dot per open action at its decision deadline
    bottom row  bars showing cash due that week
Same axis, same tick labels, aligned vertically so the reader sees that the
deadline cluster on 17 Sep sits directly above the ₹22.44 cr due that week.
That relationship is the insight and it is invisible today.

One heading for the combined chart. One legend covering both rows. One "view as
table" that shows both series. Keep the hover tooltips.

═══ PART 2 — GROUP INTO FIVE BANDS ═══
Replace the flat list of seven sections with five bands. A band is a visual
group, not just a heading — give each band a clear boundary so the eye can see
where one ends. Order matters; it is the fund manager's reading order.

BAND 1 · NOW
    the H1 situation sentence (keep as is)
    the arrival sentence (keep as is)

BAND 2 · ACT

BAND 3 · PLAN

BAND 4 · RISK

BAND 5 · EVERYTHING
    "Inbound corporate actions" — the full table. Unchanged.

MOVE "Volume versus money" OUT of the middle of the page. It is context, not

Reduce heading noise while doing this. Right now every section carries a
heading AND a subtitle. Inside a band, panels get a short label only; the band
carries the heading.

═══ PART 3 — FOUR BUGS ═══

1. BHARAT RENEWABLES SAYS IT IS IN 10 SCHEMES.
   /api/dashboard returns for topHouseExposures:
       issuer "Bharat Renewables Ltd", schemesHolding: 10
   But this issuer affects 6 of 10 schemes everywhere else in the product — the
   corporate action page, the scheme pages and the analysis view all say 6.
   Find why the house-exposure count differs and make it come from the same
   source as the rest. Add a test asserting the schemes-holding count for an
   issuer equals the count of schemes with a non-zero holding of it.

2. TWO DIFFERENT "AT STAKE" NUMBERS ON ONE PAGE.
   The stat tile shows "AT STAKE IF YOU DO NOTHING ₹7.70 cr".
   The volume chart shows "By value at stake · ₹32.63 cr".
   Same words, different definitions, no explanation, sitting on the same
   screen. Either make them the same number, or rename one so the difference is
   explicit — for example the tile is "forfeited if every entitlement lapses"
   while the chart is "total value in play across all open actions". Whichever
   you choose, the labels must make the difference obvious without the reader
   having to work it out.

3. NEGATIVE HEADROOM RENDERS AS PLAIN TEXT.
   The house-exposure table shows "-0.77%" for Bharat Renewables and "-0.20%"
   for Western Circuits. Negative headroom means the cap is BREACHED. The API
   already knows — the payload carries attention "Breach" and "Critical" — but
   the table prints bare numbers with no status treatment.
   Render the status the API supplies, using the reserved status colours, and
   keep the numeric headroom in its own column.

4. AN OVERDUE ITEM SITS IN THE QUEUE UNDECORATED.
   "What needs a look" is described as "ranked by urgency, then deadline" and
   its first row is:
       "Bundelkhand Power Ltd · Dividend · 9 days ago · 31 Aug"
   31 Aug is in the PAST. An overdue action must be visibly overdue — label it
   as such with the breach colour, and say by how long. If it should not be in
   this queue at all, exclude it and say why. Silently listing a passed
   deadline alongside future ones is the worst option.
