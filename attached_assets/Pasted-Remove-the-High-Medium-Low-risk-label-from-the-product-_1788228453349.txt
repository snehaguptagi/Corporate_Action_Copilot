Remove the High / Medium / Low risk label from the product and rank by something
that is actually computed.

The problem: "risk" is never defined anywhere. Nobody set it, no rule produces
it, and it appears as a filter on the corporate actions page and a badge on the
dashboard. Currently every one of the seven events is flagged High, so the label
carries no information and the ranking it drives is arbitrary.

Replace it with two things that are derived from real figures:

1. MATERIALITY — the largest NAV impact per unit across the affected schemes,
   in paise, which is already computed for events that have one. Display the
   number, not a bucket. Sub-paise precision matters here: 0.60 paise and 22.00
   paise are two orders of magnitude apart and both are real. Where an event
   type has no NAV impact (dividend, split, bonus are value-neutral) show the
   money figure instead and leave materiality blank rather than zero.

2. ATTENTION — a badge shown ONLY when something concrete is true:
     "Decision due in N days"     when a voluntary event needs an election
     "SEBI 10% headroom"          when exercising would breach the cap
     "Cash short"                 when the scheme cannot fund its entitlement
     "Settlement break"           when reconciliation failed
   No badge otherwise. An event that needs nothing must look like it needs
   nothing.

RANKING for the dashboard and the archive:
   first  — events needing a decision, soonest deadline first
   then   — events with a constraint or a break
   then   — everything else, by materiality descending
An event whose own reason text says "No action currently required" must never
appear at the top. That is happening now: Bharat Renewables is ranked High while
its reason reads "No action currently required".

Remove the risk field from the API, the schema, the filters and the seed. Run
`pnpm --filter @workspace/api-spec run codegen` after changing openapi.yaml.

Verify: no High/Medium/Low anywhere; ranking puts a due decision above a
settled event; an event with no constraint and no decision carries no badge.
