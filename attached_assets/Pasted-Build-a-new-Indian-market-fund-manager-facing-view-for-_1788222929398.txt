MARKET AND USER
Indian market only. INR throughout, formatted Indian style (lakh/crore grouping
like 26,40,000 and "₹22.44 cr"). The user is a FUND MANAGER at an AMC, not an
operations analyst. That changes the emphasis:
- The FM does NOT validate notice terms against the PDF. Terms arrive already
  confirmed by Operations; show a "Terms confirmed · Ops · <timestamp>" badge
  and the term values, nothing to edit.
- What the FM needs: which schemes are affected, how material it is per scheme,
  which regulatory or cash constraints bind, and what the decision costs.
- Maker-checker becomes FM (maker) -> Compliance (checker). Same rule: the
  system must refuse an approval by the person who prepared it.

NEW DOMAIN CONCEPTS (none of these exist in the codebase today)
1. Scheme: an AMC mutual fund scheme. Fields: name, SEBI category, AUM, NAV per
   unit, units outstanding (derive as AUM / NAV), available cash, holdings.
   Schemes replace the current per-event "positions"/accounts as the unit of
   impact. Add a schemes table + holdings, seeded.
2. Security master needs a last-traded price (CMP) so dilution can be computed.
3. Rights Entitlement (RE) as a separate ISIN that trades on NSE/BSE in its own
   window, earlier than the subscription deadline. "Sell rights" means selling
   REs on exchange, not an off-market action.
4. SEBI single-issuer limit: an equity scheme may not hold more than 10% of NAV
   in one company. Index schemes are exempt to the extent of index replication.
   Make the 10% a named, configurable rule, not a magic number.

CALCULATION ENGINE — add to the existing deterministic engine
Theoretical ex-rights price: TERP = (5 × CMP + 1 × 85) / 6 = ₹114.1667
Value of one right     = TERP − subscription price = ₹29.1667
Dilution per held share = CMP − TERP = ₹5.8333
Rights per scheme = floor(shares × 1 / 5)
Cash to exercise  = rights × ₹85
NAV hit per unit  = (shares × dilution) / units outstanding
Forfeited if lapsed = rights × ₹29.1667

Round securities DOWN and money to 2 dp, and use the BigInt helpers in
artifacts/api-server/src/lib/calculations.ts — not float arithmetic.

TWO CONSTRAINTS THAT MUST BIND ON THE DEFAULT PATH
Default every scheme to "exercise in full", then let the checks fire, so the
problems are visible without the user hunting for them.

1. Arka Focused 25 breaches the SEBI cap. Post-exercise it reaches 10.77% of
   NAV against the 10% limit. Maximum exercisable is 1,027,007 rights; the
   remaining 772,993 must be sold on exchange before 18 Sep. Compute this, do
   not hardcode it — post-AUM = AUM − (shares × dilution), and the cap solves
   as ((10% × postAUM) / TERP) − shares held.
2. Arka Small Cap is cash short: needs ₹2.55 cr, holds ₹1.80 cr. Affordable
   rights = floor(cash / 85) = 211,764. Short by ₹0.75 cr.
3. Arka Nifty 50 Index Fund has NO discretion — an index scheme follows the
   index provider's treatment. Render its row read-only with that explanation.

THE PAGE — five blocks, in this order, one scroll, no tabs
01 The book — all 10 schemes, AUM, NAV, holding, value, % of NAV, entitled or
   not. This is standing state and exists before any notice.
02 What arrived — issuer, ISINs, ratio, price with the discount to CMP, RE ISIN,
   ASBA, plus the six-date calendar with which dates have passed and which are
   live. Terms shown as confirmed by Ops, not editable.
03 Which schemes it reaches — a 10 → 8 → 7 → 6 funnel across the three tests
   (holds the ISIN / held on record date / folio active), then a table with one
   row per scheme showing each test and the outcome. Excluded schemes stay
   visible with their reason. This block is the trust surface: "we checked and
   it gets nothing" must read differently from "we did not look".
04 What it does to each scheme — the table above. Show the NAV hit per unit in
   paise and % of NAV with a bar against the 10% limit. State the arithmetic
   once in plain language so the numbers are checkable by hand.
05 Your decision — one row per entitled scheme with: exercise in full / exercise
   to the limit / sell all entitlements / let lapse. Show rights exercised and
   cash per row, live totals for cash to block, entitlement sale proceeds and
   value forfeited. Blocked schemes show an inline explanation naming the limit,
   the number they can actually take, and what to do with the rest. The submit
   gate stays disabled while any scheme is blocked, and says how many are.

Semantic colour is reserved for constraint status only (headroom / attention /
breach). Money figures stay neutral, so red never just means "a big number".
Use tabular numerals everywhere digits line up.
