═══ 1. THE SAME NUMBER IS COMPUTED TWO WAYS ═══
For Arka Focused 25 / Bharat Renewables, post-action issuer exposure reads:
    Analysis view      11.27%
    Scheme page        10.77%
    Decision page      10.77%
All three describe the identical thing. Analysis is wrong.

Why: Analysis values the post-rights holding at the PRE-RIGHTS price.
    1,08,00,000 × ₹120.00  ÷ ₹1,150.00 cr = 11.27%   <- what Analysis does
    1,08,00,000 × ₹114.1667 ÷ ₹1,144.75 cr = 10.77%  <- correct
After a rights issue the share price falls to the theoretical ex-rights price
(TERP ₹114.1667), and AUM falls by the dilution on the existing holding. Using
₹120 and the undiluted AUM overstates exposure.

This matters beyond cosmetics: it changes whether the cap is breached, by how
much, and therefore the maximum exercisable quantity.

Fix it by making ONE function compute issuer exposure, called by the analysis
view, the scheme page and the decision page. Do not fix the number in three
places — remove the duplicate implementations. Add a test asserting all three
surfaces return the identical figure for this scheme and issuer.
