---
name: Scheme accountability view
description: Product rules for presenting corporate actions from a fund manager's scheme-level unit of accountability.
---

Treat the scheme as the Fund Manager's primary unit of accountability. Scheme views must decompose aggregate NAV impact into per-event contributions, pair funding need with available cash and shortfall, explain SEBI headroom, and show only holdings touched by open actions.

**Why:** Portfolio totals without decomposition or available-cash context do not tell a Fund Manager where to look or whether action is required.

**How to apply:** Rank affected schemes by NAV impact, collapse unaffected schemes, link schemes and events in both directions, and keep all displayed figures derived server-side from the single scheme-impact model.
