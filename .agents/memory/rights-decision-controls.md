---
name: Rights decision controls
description: Decision and submission rules for voluntary rights issues.
---

Rights decisions must compare exercise, sell entitlement, and lapse at scheme level. Exercise quantities default to the full entitlement or the strictest permitted maximum, and the server must reject quantities above cash or concentration limits. The Fund Manager submits one complete package to Compliance.

**Why:** Per-scheme submit buttons and unquantified warning labels allowed decisions that contradicted the stated controls.

**How to apply:** Show live cash, sale, and forfeiture consequences; name the limiting rule and remainder treatment; disable aggregate submission while any row exceeds its maximum; preserve maker-checker separation.
