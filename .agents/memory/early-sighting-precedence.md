---
name: Early sighting precedence
description: How pre-custodian corporate action sightings progress into actionable cases.
---

Exchange or market sightings are indicative planning records, not actionable corporate action cases. They cannot produce elections, approvals, or instructions. A matching custodian MT564 confirms cash terms, while depository evidence remains authoritative for eligible quantity. Any disagreement must stay visible after merge.

**Why:** Fund managers need early impact awareness without creating operational instructions from unconfirmed market information.

**How to apply:** Match later notices by ISIN and event type, preserve both source records, report changed terms, and only enable controlled workflow actions after custodian confirmation.
