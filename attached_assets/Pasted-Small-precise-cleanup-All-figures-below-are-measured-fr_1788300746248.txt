═══ 2. FOUR NEAR-IDENTICAL WHITES, AND ONE FAILS CONTRAST ═══
Measured on the sidebar:
  oklab(0.971046 0.00202635 0.0048672 / 0.8)   22 uses
  oklab(0.971046 0.00202635 0.0048672 / 0.75)  22 uses
  oklab(0.971046 0.00202635 0.0048672 / 0.5)    8 uses
  rgb(255, 255, 255)                            22 uses
That is the same near-white at four opacities — Tailwind opacity modifiers
(text-white/80, /75, /50) bypassing the token set.

The /0.5 one is the problem: white at 50% opacity on the dark warm ground
rgb(42, 34, 29) will not clear 4.5:1, and it is being used for real content,
not decoration.

Replace all four with two solid tokens — a primary and a secondary foreground
for dark surfaces — each meeting 4.5:1 against rgb(42, 34, 29). No opacity
modifiers on text.
